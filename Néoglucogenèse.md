During a **long period of fasting** or in periods of **intense physical exercise**, glucose must be formed from non-carbohydrate sources; this is gluconeogenesis

Gluconeogenesis = synthesis of glucose from **non- carbohydrate precursors**. 
- The precursors are:  
    - **Lactate** 
    - **Alanine** and **glucogenic amino acids** 
    - **Glycerol** 

The 3 irreversible reactions of glycolysis; catalyzed respectively by [[glucokinase]], [[phosphofructokinase1]] and [[pyruvate kinase]]; are bypassed in gluconeogenesis by other specific.
